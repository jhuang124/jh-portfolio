Component 18

Guided Reveal Poster

A dense reference cheatsheet that reveals itself section by section. Follow the numbered hotspots in sequence, or switch to explore mode and click freely. The final poster is a complete, screenshot-worthy reference.

Usage

const sections = [
  {
    id: 'description-formula', title: 'Description Formula',
    annotation: 'Start here \u2014 this is the most important thing.',
    items: [{ label: 'WHAT', text: 'What the skill does' }, ...],
  },
  // ...more sections
];

html\`<\${GuidedRevealPoster} sections=\${sections} title="My Cheatsheet" />\`
